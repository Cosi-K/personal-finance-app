# PayStamp

**Monthly Planner** — plan where every pound of your monthly pay goes, and stamp each month into your record.

Live at [paystamp.co.uk](https://paystamp.co.uk)

## What it does

- Split your take-home pay across investments, savings, costs and guilt-free spending
- Track which bank account each payment comes from and when it goes out
- Aim for a zero-based balance, so every pound has a job
- Stamp each month to build a record you can look back on
- Sign in to save your plan and pick it up again next month

## How it's built

- `index.html` — the whole site (login screen and planner)
- Accounts and saved plans use [Supabase](https://supabase.com)
- Hosted on Cloudflare, deployed automatically from this repository
