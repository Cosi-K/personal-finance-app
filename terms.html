PayStamp Pro terms

What you get with Pro, what it costs, and how to cancel. In plain English.

Last updated 18 November 2026

The short version

  • PayStamp Pro is £1.99 a month or £19.99 a year. Prices include VAT.
  • You can try it free for 14 days without a card. The trial just ends; you’re never charged for it.
  • Cancel any time. You keep Pro until the end of the month or year you’ve paid for.
  • Changed your mind? Ask within 14 days of your first payment and you’ll get a full refund.
  • Everything that’s free in PayStamp stays free.

Who you’re dealing with

PayStamp is run by an independent sole trader in the United Kingdom, trading as PayStamp. Contact: hello@paystamp.co.uk.

Payments are taken by Stripe, which sells PayStamp Pro on our behalf under the name Onelink. That means Onelink handles your payment, VAT, receipts and payment questions, and its terms for purchases “Sold through Onelink” also apply. Your receipt and bank statement show Onelink and PAYSTAMP.CO.UK. PayStamp never sees or stores your card details.

What Pro includes

On top of everything that’s free (the Monthly Planner with stamps, Investments, Savings, Debt, Records, the Couples planner, the Compass overview and the first four Compass topics), Pro opens:

  • the other six Compass topics, with their lessons, plans and checklists
  • Insights for your whole history (free shows the last 3 months)
  • Penny, the AI helper, for up to 30 questions a day (free accounts get 3 questions to try)
  • PayStamp Hustle, for side income
  • new Pro features as we add them

We may improve or change Pro features over time. If we ever remove something important from Pro, we’ll tell you first, and you can cancel.

Price and payment

Pro costs £1.99 a month or £19.99 a year, including VAT. If you pay from outside the UK, the checkout may show the price in your own currency.

Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each month or year, using the payment method you chose, until you cancel. Onelink emails you before renewals as required.

If we change the price, we’ll email you at least 30 days before it affects you. The new price only starts at your next renewal after that, and you can cancel before then.

The free trial

Each account can try Pro free for 14 days, once. You don’t need a card, nothing is charged, and the trial ends by itself. If you’d like to keep Pro, subscribe whenever you’re ready.

Founding members

The first 100 founding members have Pro free for now, as a thank you. If that ever changes, we’ll give you at least 30 days’ notice, and you won’t be charged unless you choose to subscribe.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time. Go to PayStamp Pro › Manage or cancel your plan, or sign in at app.link.com with the email you paid with. You keep Pro until the end of the period you’ve already paid for, then your account goes back to free. Nothing is deleted: your plan and everything free carry on, and Pro pages lock again with your work kept safe inside them.

Deleting your PayStamp account doesn’t cancel a subscription on its own, so please cancel in Onelink first.

Refunds

  • Within 14 days of your first payment: if you change your mind, email hello@paystamp.co.uk or contact Onelink support, and you’ll get a full refund.
  • After that: when you cancel, you keep Pro to the end of the period you’ve paid for, and we don’t refund part-periods.
  • If something goes wrong: if you were charged by mistake or Pro didn’t work as described, tell us and we’ll put it right, including a refund where that’s fair.
  • If we close Pro or PayStamp: we’ll give you notice and refund any unused part of a yearly plan.

Nothing here affects your legal rights as a consumer in the UK.

Penny, the AI helper

Penny is an AI. She can make mistakes, so check anything important. Her answers are general guidance, not financial advice, and she can only suggest changes to your plan: nothing changes unless you tap Add. To keep PayStamp affordable, Penny has a daily limit and a monthly budget for everyone; if the budget is used up she pauses until the next month, and the rest of Pro carries on.

Not financial advice

PayStamp, including Pro, is a planning and learning tool. It never holds or moves your money. Its tips, lessons, calculators and Penny’s answers are general guidance, not personal financial advice. For advice about your own situation, speak to a regulated adviser, or try the free, impartial MoneyHelper service.

Using PayStamp fairly

Please use PayStamp for your own planning, don’t try to break or overload it, and don’t share your account. We may suspend accounts that are being misused, and we’ll tell you why.

The law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales. If you live elsewhere in the UK, you can also use your local courts. If there’s a problem, please email us first and we’ll try to sort it out.
