Questions

Sensible questions, sensible answers.

Do I need to sign up to use the app?

No. Level 1 is completely free, with no signup, no email, no card. Open the app and start using it. We only ask for payment details if you decide to unlock Level 2 and beyond.

What's free, and what costs £2.99?

Level 1 — the live planner, all the category cards, and the first three foundational lessons — is free forever. Level 2 onwards (emergency fund, full debt system, investing, and every Level 3 lesson coming next) is £2.99/month. The first 30 days are free.

What's Level 3?

The next chapter, currently being built. It covers automation, tax-efficient saving (ISAs, Pensions, LISAs), sinking funds, deeper investing, and net-worth tracking. Inside the app there's a vote where you tell me which of these you most want first — your vote shapes what gets built.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — anytime, no questions, no hoops. Click cancel and that's it. Your data stays in your browser, so cancelling doesn't delete your records.

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. Your numbers live in your browser only — nothing is sent to a server, nothing is stored remotely, no bank linking. The app works entirely on your device. You can download a backup whenever you want.

Will the app keep getting updates?

Yes — that's the whole point of the subscription. New lessons, new features, and Level 3 are all included. Subscribers get every new lesson at no extra cost.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes — it runs in your browser, so it works on phone, tablet and laptop. Because your data lives locally, each device has its own records — most people do their planning on one main device.

Who's behind this?

Just me — someone who spent years figuring this out the hard way, and built the system I wished had existed. No team, no investors, no agenda. Just an app that does the job, at a price that doesn't sting.
