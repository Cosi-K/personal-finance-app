Good to know

Can’t find your answer? Email hello@paystamp.co.uk.

Is PayStamp free?

Yes, all of it. There’s no card, no trial and nothing to upgrade to. If we ever add paid extras, we’ll tell you first, and you’ll always be able to plan your pay for free.

Do I need to link my bank account?

No. PayStamp never asks for bank details or logins. You enter your pay and payments yourself, which keeps you in control of every decision.

What does “zero-based” mean?

You give every pound of your pay a job until what’s left says £0. Bills, savings and fun all count as jobs, so nothing goes missing.

Where is my plan saved?

Your plan is saved to your PayStamp account as you go, so it’s there whenever you sign in. Other users can never see it, and you can download it or delete your account whenever you like. See our privacy page.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. PayStamp works in the web browser on your phone, tablet or computer. You can also add it to your home screen, where it opens like an app. No app store needed.

Is this financial advice?

No. PayStamp gives general guidance to help you plan. For advice about your own situation, speak to a regulated adviser or the free MoneyHelper service.

Will PayStamp email me?

Only if you turn it on: a short email on payday, and reminders for anything you’ve set a review date for. PayStamp has no adverts and never sells your data.

Can I keep a copy of my records?

Yes. Every month you stamp is kept in Records, in your account, and you can change or add months by hand there. You can also download everything as a file.

Who’s behind PayStamp?

PayStamp is built independently in the UK by someone who wanted a simpler way to plan their pay each month.
