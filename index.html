Questions

Good to know.

Can’t find your answer? Email hello@paystamp.co.uk.

Is PayStamp free?+

Yes. Create an account and start planning for free.

Do I need to link my bank account?+

No. PayStamp never asks for bank details or logins. You enter your pay and payments yourself, which keeps you in control of every decision.

What is zero-based budgeting?+

It means planning until your pay minus everything you’ve assigned equals £0. Savings, bills and fun money all count as jobs, so every pound is accounted for before the month starts.

Where is my plan saved?+

Your plan is saved to your PayStamp account as you go, so it’s there whenever you sign in. Other users can never see it, and you can download it or delete your account whenever you like. See our privacy page.

Can I use it on my phone?+

Yes. PayStamp works in the web browser on your phone, tablet or computer. You can also add it to your home screen, where it opens like an app. No app store needed.

Is this financial advice?+

No. PayStamp gives general guidance to help you plan, and Compass explains the basics in plain English. For advice about your own situation, speak to a regulated adviser or the free MoneyHelper service.

Will PayStamp email me?+

Only if you turn it on. The payday email is one short message a month, and you choose what’s in it. PayStamp has no adverts and never sells your data.

Can I keep a copy of my records?+

Yes. From Records you can save each year to its own Excel file, which opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.
