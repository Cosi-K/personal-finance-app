Why this exists

I built this because nothing else worked for me.

I tried every budgeting app. Linked my bank a hundred times. Watched the dashboards light up with categories that didn't really mean anything. Then I'd close them and nothing would change.

What worked, eventually, was something simpler. Tell every pound where to go before the month starts. Sort out debt on a real plan. Save for things you actually want. Invest a small amount, every month, forever.

The app is that system, made into a tool that's calm to use. Free for as long as you want it to be — and £2.99 a month if you want the full course behind it.

Questions

Sensible questions, sensible answers.

Do I need to sign up to use the app?

No. Level 1 is completely free, with no signup, no email, no card. You just open the app and use it. We only ask for payment details if you decide to unlock Level 2 and beyond.

What's actually free, and what costs £2.99?

Level 1 — the planner, your pots, the first three foundational lessons — is free forever. Level 2 onwards (emergency fund, full debt system, saving, investing, automation, and every future lesson I add) is £2.99/month. The first 30 days are free.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — anytime, no questions, no hoops. Click cancel in your account portal and that's it. Your data stays in your browser, so cancelling doesn't delete your records.

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. Your numbers live in your browser only — nothing is sent to a server, nothing is stored remotely, no bank linking. The app works entirely on your device. You can also download a backup of your data at any time.

Will the app keep getting updates?

Yes — that's the whole point of the subscription. New lessons, new features, and improvements roll out continuously. Subscribers get every new lesson at no extra cost.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes — it runs in your browser, so it works on phone, tablet and laptop. Open the same site on each device. (Because data lives locally, each device has its own records — most people do their planning on one main device.)

Who's behind this?

Just me — someone who spent years figuring this out the hard way, and built the system I wished had existed. No team, no investors, no agenda. Just an app that does the job, at a price that doesn't sting.
